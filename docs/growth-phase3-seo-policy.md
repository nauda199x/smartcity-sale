# Marketplace SEO landing policy

Generated marketplace landing pages are demand pages backed by current approved inventory, not arbitrary indexable filter URLs.

- phase pages require at least 2 approved listings;
- phase + unit-type pages require at least 2 approved listings;
- phase + tower pages require at least 3 approved listings;
- query-string filter pages remain utility/filter URLs rather than a programmatic index surface;
- each generated page links to the actual approved listing detail URLs and back to the relevant project information page;
- pages are regenerated on the normal inventory build cadence and therefore disappear when the corresponding live inventory cluster no longer meets the threshold.
