# SEO Content Architecture — timmuasmartcity.com

Updated: 2026-08-12

## Goal
Build topical authority around secondary-market apartment purchase intent at Vinhomes Smart City, while keeping the homepage focused on live inventory and conversion.

## Pillar / cluster structure

### P0 — Transactional homepage
- `/` — Bán căn hộ Vinhomes Smart City: quỹ căn đang bán, giá/m², lọc theo nhu cầu.
- Do not turn the homepage into a long generic article.

### P1 — Core commercial guides
1. `/blog/gia-ban-vinhomes-smart-city/` — Giá bán Vinhomes Smart City 2026: cách đọc giá và so sánh giá/m².

## Internal-link rules
- Every article links to `/` with a natural CTA to view current inventory.
- Price guide links to subdivision and property-type guides.
- Subdivision guides link to price guide + relevant property-type guides + homepage.
- Property-type guides link to price guide + subdivision comparison + homepage.
- Avoid multiple near-identical pages targeting spelling variants.

## Content rules
- Primary sources first for fixed project facts: Vinhomes / project developer / Masterise Homes.
- Current asking prices must come from the site's own live inventory when technically possible; never invent a market average.
- Distinguish asking price from final transaction price.
- Include date of update on price-sensitive pages.
- Avoid fake urgency, invented transaction counts, invented resident reviews, and unsupported investment-return promises.
- Use one clear H1, descriptive H2/H3, concise meta title/description, canonical, Article + Breadcrumb schema where appropriate.

## Initial research facts to use carefully
- Official Vinhomes material states Vinhomes Smart City is 280 ha with 14.7% construction density and is located on Thang Long Boulevard.
- Official Vinhomes material highlights the project's parks and smart-city ecosystem.
- Masterise Homes describes Masteri West Heights as located by the 4.8-ha central lake and having 22 exclusive amenities.

## First publishing wave
1. Price guide (highest commercial relevance)
2. Buying/transfer guide
3. Subdivision comparison
4. 2-bedroom buying guide
5. Sapphire guide
6. Masteri West Heights guide

Do not mass-publish dozens of thin articles. Publish a smaller connected cluster, then expand based on Search Console impressions and queries.
