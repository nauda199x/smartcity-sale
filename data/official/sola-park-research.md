# The Sola Park — editorial research log

Internal working note. Do not publish this file as a page or link it from public HTML.

## Project profile and brochure

- Project page: https://imperiasmartcity.com/vi/sola-park
- Official brochure: https://imperiasmartcity.com/upload/setting/file_brochure/1/file-brochure-1719020881.pdf
- Brochure facts used: about 46,765 m² land, about 32,129 m² landscape, five towers, 4,527 units, Studio/1PN+/2PN/2PN+/3PN, long-term ownership wording, 60-item amenity diagram, G1/G2/G3 floor plans and handover table.
- Editorial caveat: brochure travel times and renderings are marketing material, not current-state guarantees. Ownership wording must be checked against the contract and current legal documents for the exact unit.

## Three-tower package milestone

- Contractor update: https://www.coteccons.vn/first-tower-of-imperia-smart-city-the-sola-park-topped-out-marking-key-milestone-for-coteccons/
- Event date: 29 April 2025. The update calls the first topped-out tower “D3” and describes a package of three 35-storey towers plus one basement.
- Editorial caveat: do not map D3 to marketing tower G3 without a direct tower-code crosswalk.

## G5–G6 milestone

- Contractor update: https://www.ricons.vn/blog/tin-tuc/ricons-cat-noc-du-an-the-sola-park-toa-g5-g6/
- Public media post: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ricons-vn_ricons-tops-out-g5g6-towers-at-the-activity-7488508208639311872-2xNs
- Event date: 30 July 2026. G5 and G6 each have 39 above-ground floors, two basements and 744 units. The published functional split is 14 commercial/office floors plus 25 apartment floors.
- G5–G6 booklet facts used: eight elevators per tower and NFA/GFA bands for Studio, 1PN+, 2PN, 2PN+ and 3PN.
- Editorial caveat: topping out confirms the structural milestone, not handover, occupancy or completion of every interior package.
