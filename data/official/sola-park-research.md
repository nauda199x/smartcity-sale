## G5–G6 milestone

- Contractor update: https://www.ricons.vn/blog/tin-tuc/ricons-cat-noc-du-an-the-sola-park-toa-g5-g6/
- Public media post: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ricons-vn_ricons-tops-out-g5g6-towers-at-the-activity-7488508208639311872-2xNs
- Event date: 30 July 2026. G5 and G6 each have 39 above-ground floors, two basements and 744 units. The published functional split is 14 commercial/office floors plus 25 apartment floors.
- G5–G6 booklet facts used: eight elevators per tower and NFA/GFA bands for Studio, 1PN+, 2PN, 2PN+ and 3PN.
- Editorial caveat: topping out confirms the structural milestone, not handover, occupancy or completion of every interior package.


## September 2026 progress interpretation

Current public-page wording was refreshed on 2026-09-01 because the previous dossier still read too much like a generic "under construction" project.

  https://www.coteccons.vn/first-tower-of-imperia-smart-city-the-sola-park-topped-out-marking-key-milestone-for-coteccons/

  https://imperiagreenparadise.org/sola-park-mik-group-can-gio/

- Do not fabricate an exact apartment-handover date from secondary sales pages unless tied to the unit contract / developer notice.

### G5–G6
- Ricons official topping-out release, 30 July 2026:
  https://www.ricons.vn/blog/tin-tuc/ricons-cat-noc-du-an-the-sola-park-toa-g5-g6/

  - 2 basement levels.
  - 14 commercial / office floors.
  - 25 residential floors.
  - 1,488 apartments total = 744 apartments per tower.
- Earlier Ricons April 2026 progress update for the same two-tower package recorded structure at level 20 with masonry and finishing already running in parallel:
  https://www.ricons.vn/blog/tin-tuc/ricons-cap-nhat-tien-do-du-an-the-sola-park-thap-d1-d2-ha-noi-thang-4-2026/
- By the 30 July topping-out milestone, public wording can safely shift from "xây thân" to "đã cất nóc / chuyển sang hoàn thiện".

### Whole-project editorial status
- Public summary now treats The Sola Park as a five-tower project whose full skyline has formed.
- G1–G3: finishing stage.
- G5–G6: structure completed at topping-out milestone; finishing stage begins.
- Avoid saying "sắp bàn giao" as a hard promise unless a current developer notice confirms the relevant tower / customer handover schedule.
- Prefer "đang hoàn thiện" and explain remaining work: facade, MEP, waterproofing, apartment fit-out, common areas, landscape and acceptance procedures.
