## Unit mix and area bands

The official Vinhomes floor-plan content gives a clear 16-unit typical-floor mix:
- Studio: 3 units/floor, about 25.5–28.5 m².
- 1BR: 2 units/floor, about 41–45 m².
- 1BR+1: 1 unit/floor, about 49 m².
- 2BR: 5 units/floor, about 59–65 m².
- 2BR+1: 3 units/floor, about 71–74 m².
- 3BR: 2 units/floor, about 79–82 m².

The retained TK1 plan visibly enumerates CH01, CH02, CH03, CH05A, CH05, CH06, CH08A, CH08, CH09, CH10, CH11, CH12, CH12A, CH15A, CH15, CH16 = 16 positions.
TK2 retained plan also shows 16 positions on floors 31–38.

Editorial rule:
- These ranges are useful orientation, but exact transaction area comes from apartment documents.
- Be careful with thông thủy vs tim tường when comparing price/m².
