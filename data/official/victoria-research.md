### Density decision
- The retained V1–V3 typical-floor diagrams visibly enumerate 17 apartment positions around the central core.
- The working tower data also uses 612 apartments per tower, matching the published 1,836-project total across three towers.
- Some secondary pages on the market publish a different typical-floor density.
- Editorial decision: public copy says the retained **typical-floor plan shows 17 positions** rather than turning that number into a blanket claim for every special floor.

## Area-band conflict resolved by measurement convention

- Official project marketing publishes about 29–95.8 m².
- Individual tower plans can show smaller 27.x m² usable figures and larger wall-centre figures for the same product family.
- This is not automatically a factual conflict because Vietnamese apartment documents commonly show both net/usable (thông thủy) and wall-centre (tim tường) measurements.
- Editorial decision:
  - Keep 29–95.8 m² as the project-level published band.
  - Explain that tower sheets may show both measurement conventions.
  - Never compare price per square metre until both apartments use the same measurement convention.
  - The exact HĐMB of the apartment is authoritative for a transaction.

## Handover-date conflict resolved

- Several secondary sales pages still mention 2027.
- The later official MIK Group topping-out article dated 06/02/2026 says the project target is handover in 2028.
- Editorial decision: public dossier uses **2028 as the current project-level official target** and explicitly tells buyers to use the exact date in the apartment HĐMB/addendum for contractual rights and obligations.

## Current-progress wording

- Research on 01/09/2026 did not identify a higher-authority project milestone later than the official 06/02/2026 topping-out announcement.
- Editorial decision: do not invent a “September 2026 completion percentage” or claim that a specific finishing package is complete without a dated authoritative update.
- Public copy says the project is in the post-topping-out finishing phase and lists the workstreams announced by MIK Group.
