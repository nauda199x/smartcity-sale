## Data conflict intentionally not propagated

- Official project scale is 3,922 apartments.
- Common tower sheets publish about 1,170 apartments for I1 and 703 for each of I2–I5. Those figures add to 3,982, sixty units above the official project total.
- The discrepancy is consistent with a simplistic multiplication of the I1 typical-floor density across non-residential or atypical floors.
- Editorial decision: do not publish an I1 apartment total or sum the tower sheets. Keep 3,922 as the project total and present tower floors, typical density, lifts and unit bands only. The public page explains why it does not show a tower-total column.
