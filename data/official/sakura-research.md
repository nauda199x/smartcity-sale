## Important naming/unit-count conflict

### Early legal disclosure
- https://smartcity.vinhomes.vn/bai-viet/cong-bo-thong-tin-du-an-bds-vinhomes-smart-city-phan-khu-the-sakura/
- Also mirrored on:
  https://vinhomes.vn/vi/cong-bo-thong-tin-du-an-bds-vinhomes-smart-city-phan-khu-the-sakura
- The disclosure lists:
  - Z37.1 (SA1): 37 floors, 680 apartments
  - U38.1 (SA4): 38 floors, 1,104 apartments
  - Z38M.1 (SA2): 38 floors, 699 apartments
  - U39.1 (SA3): 39 floors, 1,103 apartments
- Later/current commercial material uses SA1, SA2, SA3 and SA5 instead of SA4.
- This source set does not contain a formal rename document proving that SA4 and SA5 are legally the same block.
- Later commercial-market pages also publish different apartment totals for SA2 and SA5.

### Editorial decision
- Public navigation follows current commercial naming: SA1 / SA2 / SA3 / SA5.
- Do not sum or present a “precise total apartments” figure from incompatible generations of documents.
- Do not silently map the SA4 legal count to SA5.
- For buying decisions, use exact tower/apartment documents, HĐMB and current building records.
- The public page explains this conflict as a data-quality note without exposing outbound source links.
