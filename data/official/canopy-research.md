## Tower design baseline

### Overall
- Three towers use Z-type residential floor plates.
- Typical floor plan material consistently shows 16 apartment positions.
- 7 elevators is the market-published tower standard; secondary sources usually describe 6 passenger lifts + 1 service lift.
- Corridor width commonly published as 1.8 m.
- Public copy may use:
  - 16 apartments/typical floor,
  - 38 floors,
  - 586 apartments/tower from the legal completion notice.
- Exact elevator count remains a design/market-published specification rather than a field extracted from the 2025 legal acceptance notice.

## Area-band conflict / editorial rule

Different generations of marketing documents publish different project-level bands:
- about 28.8–92.3 m² on many project summaries,
- some pages extend 3BR+1 gross-area examples to about 100.9 or 109 m²,
- TC3 later material includes smaller Studio values around 25.4–25.5 m² and 3BR around 83 m².

Editorial decision:
- Do not present one narrow project-wide range as if every tower shares it.
- Public project page says the project spans from mid-20s m² Studio products to 90+ m² large family units depending on tower and measurement convention.
- Exact transaction area must come from the apartment-specific documents.
- Price/m² comparisons require the same area convention.

## Location / boundaries

Current project-market descriptions consistently place The Canopy:
- between/adjacent to The Tonkin and Masteri West Heights,
- near school land,
- close to Central Park and Smart City internal amenities,
- with access toward Lê Trọng Tấn / major western-Hanoi roads.

A commonly repeated tower-level boundary for TC3:
- northeast: Masteri West Heights internal park,
- southeast: TC2,
- southwest: school land,
- northwest: The Tonkin internal park.

Editorial rule:
- Public copy focuses on operating road/amenity access.
- Future metro references, if any, are labelled long-term planning context rather than current infrastructure.

## Completion / operating-state wording

The strongest current milestone is the official 04/07/2025 completion-inspection acceptance notice.
Therefore:
- 2026 public page should not say “dự kiến bàn giao”.
- It may state that the three towers passed the state completion-inspection milestone in July 2025.
- The site can describe current real photos as completed/operational environment.
- Do not invent occupancy rate, certificate issuance rate, service fee, parking entitlement or current market price without fresh building/apartment evidence.
